## Context

MedCheck is an accessibility-minded HackRU 2026 medication companion prototype. It extracts label information from a photo and compares the name and strength with a tracked medication record.

## Approach

The OCR-first workflow distinguishes matching, mismatched, uncertain, and dose-status states. Ambiguous information prompts review or rescan; dose logging requires a separate explicit confirmation. The repository describes a web experience and an iOS preview.

## Scope and limitations

A label scan is evidence about a bottle, not proof that a medication was taken. This is a prototype, not a clinical decision system.
