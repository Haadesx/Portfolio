## Context

During my internship at **Arcascope**, I worked on quantifying and predicting daily fatigue from wearable data. Fatigue is subjective and noisy, which makes standard regression prone to overfitting.

## Methodology

We treated it as an **ordinal regression** problem instead of plain regression or classification.

### What we changed

1. **CORAL (Consistent Rank Logits) layer:**
   We replaced the standard output layer with a CORAL layer so the model respects the ordered fatigue scores (e.g. a 1–10 scale).

2. **Custom loss:**
   Combined `CornLoss` (Conditional Ordinal Regression) with standard MSE to penalize large deviations while respecting rank order.

3. **Physics-informed features:**
   Integrated **Runge-Kutta** methods to model circadian rhythm dynamics directly in the feature set.

## Results vs Baseline

We iterated through ~20 architectures. The final model beat the baseline by a wide margin:

| Metric | Baseline (Linear Reg) | Deep Ordinal Model | Improvement |
|:-------|:---------------------|:-------------------|:------------|
| MAE    | 1.17                 | **0.25**           | **78%**     |
| Accuracy| 52% (within ±1)     | **85%**            | +33%        |

That MAE drop let the system send users a daily alert they could act on.
