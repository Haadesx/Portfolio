title: "ICONOCLAST: LLM Representation Editing"
---

## Context

ICONOCLAST is a Python framework for researching refusal-behavior editing in open-weight language models.

## Approach

The framework combines contrastive residual activations, benign-subspace preservation, projection edits, and Optuna-based parameter search.

## Evaluation

The work benchmarked 10 matched model comparisons using refusal, benign-overrefusal, and KL-divergence metrics. Six edited model artifacts were released on Hugging Face.

## Scope

These comparisons describe a research framework and its reported evaluation; they do not establish general safety or performance across all models and tasks.
