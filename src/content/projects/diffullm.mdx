title: "DiffuLLM: Discrete Diffusion for Text"
---

## Context

DiffuLLM is Varesh Patel’s system in a collaborative comparative study of three discrete diffusion language models trained on recipe corpora.

## Approach

A bidirectional transformer uses D3PM-style absorbing-mask corruption and masked reconstruction. Inference iteratively unmasks tokens to refine a generated sequence.

The model was trained for over 500,000 iterations using PyTorch Distributed Data Parallel and SLURM. Experiments compared sampling settings.

## Scope and limitations

The work is a research comparison on recipe data; results should be interpreted within that domain and experimental setup. Long-context repetition and global coherence remain documented failure modes.
