---

## Context

CareWatch is a Bitcamp 2026 hackathon MVP exploring video-assisted detection of concerning caregiver interactions. The résumé lists it as second prize for Best Social Good Hack.

## Approach

A Python pipeline combines YOLOv8 pose-based heuristics, vision-model review, a rolling 30-second clip buffer, event logging, and push alerts. A React dashboard supports live monitoring and recording review, with an iOS guardian client.

## Scope and limitations

The system is an MVP intended to surface evidence for human review, not proof of abuse or a validated safety system.
