## Context

HealthTwin is a healthcare decision workspace for provider operators and capital stakeholders. It turns uploaded financial, operational and market evidence into assumptions, scenarios and decision briefs.

## Approach

A FastAPI backend and React frontend coordinate evidence ingestion, scenario runs and memo generation. Shared domain packages and evaluation scaffolding support repeatable workflows.

## Scope and limitations

The repository documents a deterministic demo and says the full decision methodology and weighting logic are not published.
